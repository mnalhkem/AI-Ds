Students identify AI systems they interact with every day and classify them: Siri
What AI does: AI allows computer systems to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence and understanding.
What data AI uses: My voice, location, information from my device and settings, and data from the World Wide Web.
What business problem it solves: It makes it easier and faster for people to get information without having to manually look for it; this improves convenience.
Who benefits: All users benefit because they can complete tasks quicker, and businesses also benefit because AI assistants make their services easier.
What could go wrong: Private information may get exposed if permissions are not handled properly.
